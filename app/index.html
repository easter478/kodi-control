Live view

Live

Kodi's own screenshots of the TV, pulled over SSH. Menus and the OSD show; on Amlogic boxes (CoreELEC) the video itself sits on a hardware plane and usually comes out black. "As fast as possible" takes the next shot the moment the last one arrives; each frame is still a few hundred ms old because Kodi has to render, encode and send a PNG. Real zero-delay video needs an HDMI capture card on the PC.

Connect, then open this page to see the TV.
