Seek preview heavy on the box

Like Netflix or Prime Video: while you seek, a box above the progress bar shows the frame you are about to jump to, with its time. Seeking waits for the seek delay, so you can press several times and watch the preview before it jumps. Frames are made on the box by ffmpeg, cached, and fetched first around where you are watching. Works for files on the box or a USB drive, SMB and NFS shares (read through Kodi itself, so no share password is needed) and http streams.

Live view

Live
Video heavy

Kodi's own screenshots of the TV, pulled over SSH. Menus and the OSD show; on Amlogic boxes (CoreELEC) the video itself sits on a hardware plane and usually comes out black. "As fast as possible" takes the next shot the moment the last one arrives; each frame is still a few hundred ms old because Kodi has to render, encode and send a PNG. Turn on Video to stream the video plane itself (Amlogic only, read from /dev/amvideocap0 on the box): it plays live at up to 25 fps under the menus and OSD. Real zero-delay full quality still needs an HDMI capture card.
